Model Evolution & Roadmap

From baseline to our best model, and what comes next.

✓

Baseline — Uni-Sign + mT5

Full fine-tuning of Uni-Sign encoder with mT5-base decoder. 256 temporal tokens, no compression.
BLEU-4: 2.38
Tokens: 256
Params: ~363M
✓

Compression Only

Added temporal compression (Conv1D stride=2) to reduce 256→128 tokens. Initial stage showed poor results without contrastive alignment.
BLEU-4: 0.53
Tokens: 136
Issue: Undertrained
✓

Stage B — Compression + Staged Training

Freeze encoder, train compression → then fine-tune all. Multi-stage curriculum improved BLEU beyond baseline.
BLEU-4: 2.94
BLEU-4 KK: 2.55
Tokens: 136
●

Compression + VAP-lite BEST

Added in-batch contrastive alignment (InfoNCE) between compressed and original representations. Best Russian BLEU, strong overall improvement.
BLEU-4: 2.88
BLEU-4 RU: 3.54 (+29%)
ChrF RU: 26.03
VAP Cosine: 0.14
1

SWA Ensemble PLANNED

Stochastic Weight Averaging over checkpoint ensemble. Already attempted (30h run), marginal gains expected. TTA via frame subsampling.
Expected Δ: +0.1~0.3 BLEU
2

Multimodal Fusion FUTURE

Add RGB stream (ViT/CVT) alongside pose. Cross-attention between visual and skeletal features. Expected to help with facial expression and mouth movement disambiguation.
3

Larger Decoder FUTURE

Upgrade from mT5-base (220M) to mT5-large (740M) or mT5-XXL (13B, QLoRA). Full fine-tuning or LoRA adaptation for better Cyrillic generation.
